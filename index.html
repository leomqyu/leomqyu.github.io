Biography

I'm Muquan Yu (喻牧泉). I am currently a Ph.D. student in School of Computing and Data Science, The University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Yizhou Yu. Prior to that, I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science from The Chinese University of Hong Kong. My research interests are broadly in Machine Learning, with a particular interest in biologically inspired AI systems, computer vision, and language modeling.
